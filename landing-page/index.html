O'Keeffe Experiments
The following prototypes are for experimental purposes and are using information that has not been reviewed.

Ways into O'Keeffe's work

Prototypes from the Digital Experience team at the Georgia O'Keeffe Museum that turn collection data into things you can explore, from maps and timelines to works shown at their true size. Each is built in p5.js, a creative coding tool made for artists, with the help of generative AI. Try any of them, then tell us what you think.

Which Way Is Up?

Game, 2026Six works a gameTwo in each with a disputed orientation

Some of O'Keeffe's works have hung sideways, or even upside down. Museums, publishers, and O'Keeffe's own records don't always agree on which way is up. Turn each work until it looks right to you, hang it, and see who agrees with you.

O'Keeffe Experiments is from the Digital Experience team at the Georgia O'Keeffe Museum. These prototypes use data and images from Access O'Keeffe, including the catalogue raisonné, along with some subject information added with the help of AI. Because these are prototypes, the data and images have not been reviewed for publication in this format.
