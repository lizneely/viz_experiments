O'Keeffe Experiments
The following prototype is for experimental purposes and is using information that has not been reviewed.

O'Keeffe in Context

Moments from O'Keeffe's life, from the Georgia O'Keeffe Museum's timeline records, set beside events in the arts and history. Her life runs along the top strand, with arts events and history beneath. Key moments are labeled above the timeline and major eras are shaded; outlined labels and hollow marks are added context from outside the Museum's records. Zoom in and more labels find room. Drag to pan, scroll or use + and − to zoom, and click a mark or label to pin its story below, along with a work from the catalogue raisonné made that year.
