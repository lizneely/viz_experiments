O'Keeffe Experiments
The following prototype is for experimental purposes and is using information that has not been reviewed.

O'Keeffe to Scale

Every dated painting, watercolor, pastel, and drawing in the catalogue raisonné, drawn as an outline at its true size relative to the others, all sharing one corner. Color marks the decade. Press Play to watch her scale change year by year, or choose By subject to compare the sizes of her landscapes, flowers, bones, abstractions, and more side by side.
